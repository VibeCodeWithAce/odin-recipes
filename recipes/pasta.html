My Grandma's Pasta Recipe

Grandma's Recipe:

This recipe has been passed down from generations to generations.

It is a simple Italian recipe that my family perfected.

Ingredients:

  • Bell Peppers
  • Pasta
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Chilli Sauce
  • Salt, Seasoning, Hot Spice

Steps:

  1. Boil your pasta for 7mins
  2. Chop all tomatoes, peppers, onions and get them ready to fry
  3. Fry them on low heat and add the seasonings while they fry
  4. Finally, turn in your boiled pasta and stir fry for 4mins
  5. Serve hot with cool juice of choice
